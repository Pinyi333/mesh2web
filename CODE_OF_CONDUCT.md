# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want mesh2web to be a welcoming space for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

- Be kind, patient and constructive. Critique ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, or
  sustained disruption.
- Assume good intent, and accept correction gracefully.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/pinyi333/mesh2web/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove content, or warn, suspend or ban contributors, following
the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
